Transformer Vector Basics

An arrow for every phase. Read the arrows, call the meter.

Step 1

An arrow is just a sine wave going around

Each phase is an arrow spinning 60 times a second. The height of the arrow tip is the voltage right now. Trace that height over time and you get the sine wave. Three phases means three arrows, always 120° apart.

Slowed way down. Real 60 Hz is too fast to see.

The trick: freeze the arrows and you get a vector diagram. The diagram is a snapshot that shows how big each voltage is (arrow length) and how far apart in time they are (the angle).

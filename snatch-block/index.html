Work it like the test

You get the rigging picture and a blank organizer. Nothing gets filled in for you. Carry your own numbers down the line out column, lock it in, then go back to Block 1 for the tensions. Your sheet gets graded at the end.

On the test, draw this first

  1. Number the blocks from the load out. Block 1 holds the load.
  2. Write two lists: Line outs on top, SBTs underneath.
  3. Pass 1: get every line out. Block 1: the load × 1.1. Every block after that: the line out above × 1.1. No SBTs yet.
  4. Pass 2: once all line outs are done, go back to Block 1. Factor, then SBT, for each block.
SBT₂ uses LO₂. Same row, every time.

Haul line: parted blocks

  1. Count P: the parts of rope holding up the load. The haul line itself does not count.
  2. Friction: 1.1ᴾ. That's 1.1 multiplied by itself P times, 10% for every part.
  3. Pull: 1.1ᴾ × W, then ÷ P. That's the haul line tension.
1,000 lbs on 2 parts: 1.21 × 1,000 ÷ 2 = 605 lbs
